Prof. Sungjin Lee
and
Prof. Jisung Park
. We are part of the
Department of Computer Science and Engineering
at
Pohang University of Science and Technology (POSTECH)
.
​
Our group aims to conduct cutting-edge research and education in a broad range of computer systems, including computer architecture, operating systems, system security, and hardware/software cooperation. The overarching goal of our research is to design fast, secure, and cost-effective computing systems that have positive impact on our society and human lives.
