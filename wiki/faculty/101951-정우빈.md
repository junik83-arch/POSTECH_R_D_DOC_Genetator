Neural Interface
Neural interfaces aim to establish a direct and high-fidelity communication link between neural networks and electronic systems. By integrating high-density microelectrode arrays on CMOS chips, this research enables large-scale recording and stimulation of neural activity, providing insights into neural computation, connectivity, and brain-inspired information processing.
DNA Data Storage
DNA data storage explores the use of synthetic DNA as an ultra-dense and long-term information medium. By coupling DNA synthesis and readout with semiconductor technologies, this field seeks to encode digital information directly into DNA sequences in a massively parallel and programmable manner, opening new paradigms for future data storage and molecular computing.
Electrochemical Sensor
Electrochemical sensors based on CMOS platforms enable sensitive and real-time detection of biochemical signals at the cellular and molecular levels. By integrating electrochemical transducers with on-chip electronics, this research supports scalable, multiplexed biosensing for applications such as neural chemistry monitoring, cellular metabolism analysis, and bio-electronic interfaces.
