## 연구키워드
Anaerobic Digestion, Biodegradable Plastics, Bioconversion, Biokinetics, Microbial Community Analysis, Artificial Intelligence

## 주요성과
￭ Development and statistical optimization of environmental bioprocesses ￭ Microbial dynamics in carbon-and nitrogen-removal systems using QPCR & other molecular methods ￭ Selected microbial community to enhance anaerobiosis ￭ Bio-recycling of organic waste to value-added product (mycelia)

## 학회발표
오염된 생분해성 플라스틱 배달용기의 처리 시나리오에 따른 에너지 소비, 온실가스 배출 및 비용 비교 분석 (2025년 유기성자원학회 추계학술대회); 음식물류폐기물 종량제봉투의 생분해성플라스틱으로 대체: polylactic-acid 와 음식물의 통합 혐기소화 특성과 산업화 가능성 (2025년 유기성자원학회 추계학술대회); Predominance and pathway expression of lignin-degrading bacteria enriched from wastewater treatment plant (11th IWA Membrane Technology Conference (MTC 2025)); Characteristics of industrial wastewater from pulp and paper processing (11th IWA Membrane Technology Conference (MTC 2025)); Effect of Inocula from Full-Scale Anaerobic Plants on Thermophilic Bio-gasification of PLA and PBAT (12th International Conference on Sustainable Solid Waste Management); Anaerobic co-digestion of food waste leachate and PLA flake (3RINCs (The 3R international scientific conference on material cycles and waste management)); Bioaugmentation to improve anaerobic digestion of fish waste: Feed-to-microbes ratio, augmentation dosage and kinetic analysis (3RINCs (The 3R international scientific conference on material cycles and waste management)); Comparative analysis of removal efficiency and microbial communities in thermophilic anaerobic digestion of biodegradable plastic (PBAT) (3RINCs (The 3R international scientific conference on material cycles and waste management)); Pilot-scale Digestion of Thermo-alkali Pretreated Polylactic Acid (3RINCs (The 3R international scientific conference on material cycles and waste management)); Utilization of Machine Learning Regression Models for Anaerobic Digestion Process Recovery Under Repeated Pulse Inputs of Fish Waste (3RINCs (The 3R international scientific conference on material cycles and waste management)); Biogasification of polyhydroxyalkanoates with various organic waste (3RINCs (The 3R international scientific conference on material cycles and waste management)); Effect of Initial Microbial Community Differences on PLA Bio-gasification in Thermophilic Anaerobic condition (3RINCs (The 3R international scientific conference on material cycles and waste management)); Transformer based Multi-step Time Series Forecasting of Methane Yield in Full-Scale Anaerobic Digestion (3RINCs (The 3R international scientific conference on material cycles and waste management)); Forecasting Methane Yield in Anaerobic Digestion Systems Using Representation Learning (3RINCs (The 3R international scientific conference on material cycles and waste management)); Treatability of recombinant T4 bacteriophage lysozymes with secondary sludge (3RINCs (The 3R international scientific conference on material cycles and waste management)); AI를 이용한 혐기성소화 공정 진단 (2025년 유기성자원학회 춘계학술대회); Anaerobic digestion of food waste as a sustainable solution: energy assessment and application to biodegradable plastics (SEGT 2024); Machine learning algorithms to indicate anaerobic process stability with pulse input (SEGT 2024); Reconstruction.based Learning Approach to Detect Instability of Anaerobic Process: An Early Warning Detection (SEGT 2024); Transformer-based anaerobic digestion time series forecasting model: inference to interpretation (10th International symposium on energy from biomass and waste (VENICE 2024)); 혐기성 소화조의 start-up 및 안정화 단계의 공정효율 해석 (2024년 대한환경공학회 국내학술대회); Characteristics of microbial community structure in the aeration tank of pulp and paper (P&P) treatment (2024년 대한환경공학회 국내학술대회); Transformer 기반 통합 소화조 바이오가스 생산량 예측 및 해석 (2024년 대한환경공학회 국내학술대회); 고단백 유기성 폐자원의 혐기성 소화조 start.up 전략 (유기성자원학회 2024년도 춘계학술대회); 열-알칼리 전처리후 바이오플라스틱의 음식물-하수슬러지 통합소화 (한국폐기물자원순환학회 2024년도 춘계학술대회); Polylactic acid (PLA)의 혐기성 소화에서 미생물 군집 변화에 관한 연구 (한국환경에너지공학회 2023년 추계학술대회); Polylactic acid 및 polybutylene adipate terephthalate의 연속식 통합소화 (2023년 유기성자원학회 추계학술대회); 난분해성 물질의 혐기성 소화 및 공정제어 기술 (2023년 유기성자원학회 추계학술대회); 4차 산업시대 혐기성소화 공정 제어 플랫폼 (2023년 대한환경공학회 국내학술대회); Shifts in Microbial Diversity of Anaerobic Digester after High Loading of Fish Waste (2023 Asia-Pacific International Society of Microbial Electrochemistry and Technology (AP-ISMET))
