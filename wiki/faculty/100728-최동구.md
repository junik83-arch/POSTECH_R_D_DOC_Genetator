System & Economic Decision Analytics (SEA) Lab.
Welcome to
SEA Lab
. in the Department of Industrial and Management Engineering at POSTECH !
We
aim to provide better decision-making strategies for several types of stakeholders based on the better understanding of future dynamics in complex systems and interactions among stakeholders. Our approaches are mainly based on various techniques from optimization, stochastic process, and also economics.
Particularly, we have been conducting researches about
“Business Operation/Policy Design”
and
“Sequential/Multi-agent Decision System Operation”
based on (1) Optimization models under uncertainty (stochastic programming, robust optimization),  (2) sequential decision process models (dynamic programming, reinforcement learning), and (3) multi-agent system models (game theory, multi-agent learning).
Research Topics
BUSINESS OPERATOIN DESIGN
새로운 비즈니스 모델, 정책의 도입과 관련해 운영 구조를 설계 및 최적화하고
상품/서비스 가격을 책정하는 연구 분야

시스템에서의 최적 의사결정 및 시스템 운영 방안을 연구하는 분야
