## 주요성과
￭ First realization of epitaxial T-Nb2O5 thin films exhibiting ultrafast resistivity switching ￭ First demonstration of all-oxide metasurfaces enabled by synchronized local ionic gating ￭ Electrical manipulation of electronic and magnetic phases in epitaxial oxide nanostructures ￭ Atomistic mechanism of nanoparticle exsolution in oxide thin films ￭ Emergent room-temperature multiferroicity from morphotropic phase mixtures ￭ Electrically switchable photovoltaic effects in narrow-bandgap ferroelectrics
