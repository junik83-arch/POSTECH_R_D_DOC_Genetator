We computationally design proteins to program how they self-assemble into complex nanostructures.
We design protein nanostructures with useful structural, chemical, and biological properties. Based on chemical & physical principles, geometric principles, and machine learning, we design proteins
computationally
and validate them
experimentally
. By iterating computational and experimental approaches, we improve protein design methods and develop new design principles.
