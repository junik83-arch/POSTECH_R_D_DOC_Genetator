## 주요성과
￭ Synthesis of new type of artificial aquaporin water channels (Nature Nanotechnology 2020) ￭ Scalable biomimetic membrane developments using cellular membrane porins for water treatment (Nature Materials 2020) ￭ Atmospheric water harvesting device that harvest water from Death Valley desert air using MOFs (Nature Water 2023) ￭ Thin film composite gas separation membranes that show exceptional molecular sieving properties to seaprate light gases including helium, hydrogen, carbon dioxide, oxygen, nitrogen, and methane (Chemistry of Materials 2022) ￭ Membrane developments using artificial water channels: The first full synthetic biomimetic membrane for water separations and purification (Nature Communications 2018)
