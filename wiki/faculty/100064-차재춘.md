PK

Topology Workshop (2022 Fall) focuses on geometric topology and geometric group theory in a broad sense. It started as a part of collaboration efforts among POSTECH, KAIST and KIAS, and hopefully, more institutions are involved. It is designed to have talks of current interests by participants, informal and over-the-walk style explanation of the topics and the current state of the arts. It encourages intensive discussion and collaboration during the workshop.
