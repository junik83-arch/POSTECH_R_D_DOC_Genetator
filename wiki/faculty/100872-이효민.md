> 이효민 교수는 포항공과대학교 화학공학과 부교수로 재직 중이며, 연성 물질 및 기능성 계면 연구실(SMFIL)을 이끌고 있습니다. 연구실에서는 계면에서의 연성 물질 구조 및 동역학을 이해하고 제어하여 새로운 기능성 재료를 개발하는 것을 목표로 합니다. 주요 연구 분야는 계면 공학, 미세 유체 역학, 암호화 및 디스플레이, 인공 세포, 헬스케어 및 지속 가능성, 습윤성, 하이드로겔, 미세 패턴 형성, 나노섬유, 다상 에멀젼 등입니다. 이효민 교수는 2026년 젊은 과학자상(Y-KAST)을 수상하는 등 다수의 학술상 수상 경력이 있으며, 한국화학공학회 및 한국고분자학회 등에서 활발한 학회 활동을 하고 있습니다.

Use soft things to solve hard problems
The overall aim of our research is to understand and control the structure and dynamics of soft matter at interfaces in dimensions ranging from nano- to macroscale and engineer new functional materials. A key aspect of our research is the preparation of well-defined molecules, materials, emulsions and surfaces, which leads to desired function.

Wetting
The ability to control surface wettability plays an important role in many applications, from antifogging and self-cleaning surfaces to enhancing condensation. By manipulating surface energy and microstructural design, we aim to develop hydrophilic and hydrophobic surfaces tailored for specific functionalities. These designs enable the effective management of water droplets, leading to advancements in antifogging coatings, liquid-repellent surfaces, and more.
Hydrogel
Hydrogels, known for their high water content and tunable mechanical properties, serve as versatile materials in designing functional interfaces. We explore their applications in creating antifogging layers, responsive coatings, and soft actuators. By adjusting chemical compositions and crosslinking densities, we tailor hydrogels to exhibit specific responses to environmental stimuli such as humidity and temperature, broadening their utility across diverse fields.
Micropatterning
Micropatterning techniques enable precise spatial control over material properties and surface functionalities. Using advanced methods like lithography, we fabricate microstructured surfaces that enhance optical transparency, mechanical stability, and environmental resilience. These patterned surfaces find applications in flexible electronics, optical devices, and multifunctional coatings that integrate hydrophobic and hydrophilic regions seamlessly.
Nanofibers
Electrospinning is a versatile technique for generating continuous nanofibers with high surface area, enhancing the wettability of materials. These nanofiber matrices exhibit a high surface area, interconnected pores, and thin permeation paths. By exploiting these properties, we aim to develop high-performance membranes with superior permeability for various applications.
