## 연구관심분야
￭ Topological superconductivity: realization and detection of non-Abelian quasiparticles ￭ Twisted/Rhombohedral multilayer graphene physics: investigation of emergent correlated phases ￭ STM and quantum transport integration: correlating atomic-scale structure with macroscopic transport properties
