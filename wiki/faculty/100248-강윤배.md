In the Clean Steel Laboratory (CSL) of the Graduate Institute of Ferrous and Eco Materials Technology and jointly associated with Department of Materials Science and Engineering, Pohang University of Science and Technology, we are aiming to contribute to our academic community and steel industry by carrying out research, education, and communication.  The main research area of interest is chemical metallurgy, including chemical thermodynamics, kinetics, and solidification.  The practical application area includes the pyrometallurgical process, in particular for steelmaking and continuous casting processes.
We are looking for students and post-docs who are interested in high-temperature process metallurgy including molten steel cleanliness (chemical refining and inclusion control), ferrous scrap and Li-ion battery recycling, hydrogen production, hydrogen ironmaking, low C emission steel production via chemical metallurgical principles (thermodynamics, kinetics, physicochemical properties of melts, etc.). If you are interested in carrying out the research at CSL, please contact Dr. Youn-Bae Kang.
