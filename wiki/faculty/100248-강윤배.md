> 강윤배 교수는 포항공과대학교 철강대학원 및 신소재공학과 소속의 Clean Steel Laboratory(CSL)를 이끌고 있으며, 2009년에 에콜 폴리테크닉 드 몽레알에서 CSL에 합류했습니다. 그의 주요 연구 분야는 화학 야금으로, 화학 열역학, 반응 속도론, 응고를 포함하며, 제강 및 연속 주조 공정과 같은 고온 공정 야금에 중점을 둡니다. 강 교수는 2025년 ICS 학회에서 '지속 가능한 철강 품질 관리를 위한 철강 청정도/개재물 관련 기초 지식의 현재 상태'라는 제목으로 기조 강연을 했으며, CALPHAD 2025를 성공적으로 조직했습니다. 현재 'Recycling Advisory Council', '반응성 가스를 활용한 전기로 용강의 탈질 개선 기초 연구' 등 다수의 연구 프로젝트를 수행하고 있습니다.

In the Clean Steel Laboratory (CSL) of the Graduate Institute of Ferrous and Eco Materials Technology and jointly associated with Department of Materials Science and Engineering, Pohang University of Science and Technology, we are aiming to contribute to our academic community and steel industry by carrying out research, education, and communication.  The main research area of interest is chemical metallurgy, including chemical thermodynamics, kinetics, and solidification.  The practical application area includes the pyrometallurgical process, in particular for steelmaking and continuous casting processes.
We are looking for students and post-docs who are interested in high-temperature process metallurgy including molten steel cleanliness (chemical refining and inclusion control), ferrous scrap and Li-ion battery recycling, hydrogen production, hydrogen ironmaking, low C emission steel production via chemical metallurgical principles (thermodynamics, kinetics, physicochemical properties of melts, etc.). If you are interested in carrying out the research at CSL, please contact Dr. Youn-Bae Kang.

History
Clean Steel Laboratory (CSL) was established in 2005 as one of first laboratories of Graduate Institute of Ferrous Technology (GIFT), Pohang University of Science and Technology (POSTECH).  It was transferred from Department of Materials Science and Engineering, POSTECH, led by Dr. Hae-Geon Lee.
Dr. Henry Gaye joined this laboratory after a long time working at IRSID, France in 2005.
Dr. Sung-Mo Jung joined this laboratory from GSIST, POSTECH in 2005.
Dr. Sang-Joon Kim joined this laboratory from POSCO in 2007.
Dr. Henry Gaye retired in 2009, and came back to France.
Dr. Sung-Mo Jung moved to Environmental Metallurgy Laboratory (EML), GIFT, POSTECH.
Dr. Youn-Bae Kang joined this laboratory from Ecole Polytechnique de Montreal, Canada in 2009.
Dr. Hae-Geon Lee retired in 2012, and was appointed as "Professor Emeritus".
Dr. Jung-Wook Cho joined this laboratory from POSCO in 2012.
Dr. Sang-Joon Kim resigned in 2014.
Dr. Jung-Wook Cho moved to Casting Technology Laboratory (CTL), GIFT, POSTECH in 2016.
Dr. Joo Choi joined this laboratory from POSCO in 2020.
