# 이병훈 (전자전기공학과)

## 기본 정보
- 학과: [전자전기공학과](../domain/전자전기공학과.moc.md)

- 홈페이지: <http://eesl.postech.ac.kr/>

## 연구관심분야
- Extreme low power computing: novel devices and architectures that can drastically reduce the power consumption in the information processing systems and sensors
- 2D material based photo detectors and memory devices derived from the novel material based device research

## 연구키워드
3D integration, packaging, 2D material, Nano-composite thin film, Computer modeling, Novel devices and architecture, 3DIC semiconductor, Infrared sensor, nanoinfra

## 주요성과
- Contributed to the development of metal gate/high-k dielectric during 1997-2007
- Commercialization of novel technologies related to semiconductor and sensors
- In-depth study of graphene based devices and applications
- Demonstrated various novel applications of 2D Materials and nano-composite thin film
- Current works on silicon device and reliability
