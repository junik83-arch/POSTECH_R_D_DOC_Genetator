## 연구관심분야
￭ Market analysis to examine an existence of market power exercised by economic agents along the supply chain of agricultural commodities or food products. ￭ Utilization of online microdata to conduct event studies in a timely manner and mitigate information asymmetry. ￭ Investment decision making under uncertainty. ￭ Analysis of government policies related to agriculture, food, and trade. ￭ Automation of collecting and processing data for disseminating knowledge and information with higher frequency.

## 주요성과
￭ Recovery of firm-level data from market-level data by utilizing optimization and MPEC (Mathematical Programming with Equilibrium Constraints). ￭ Proving that spatially confined agricultural markets can a source of market power in agricultural commodity procurement. ￭ Applying deep-learning approach for non-parametrics analysis for investigating pricing strategy in food service industry.

Research Interests
Industrial Organization
Industrial Organization is a branch of economics that explores how businesses strategically behave, how markets are shaped, and how these forces impact prices, innovation, and consumers.
Social Sciences Data Analytics
Social Sciences Data Analytics uses data to understand human behavior, society, and policy. By combining statistics, mathematical modeling, computing, and social science insights, it turns complex information into evidence that can guide decisions and create positive change.
Food/Agricultural Economics
Food/Agricultural Economics studies how food is produced, distributed, and consumed, and how these processes affect farmers, markets, and society. It explores issues like food prices, trade, sustainability, and rural development, helping shape policies and business decisions that support both producers and consumers.
