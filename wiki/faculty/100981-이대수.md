◽ Pioneering the Future of Complex Oxide Epitaxial Thin Films
◽
At ACOL, our journey begins at the frontier of condensed matter physics & materials science. We dive deep into the realm of complex oxide epitaxial thin films, a class of materials holding promises that have the potential to reshape our technological landscape.
◽
Why Complex Oxides?
◽
Complex oxides, particularly those based on transition metals, are a treasure trove of multifaceted properties. These materials can exhibit a rich variety of physical behaviors, from ferroelectricity and magnetism to superconductivity and unconventional transport. Such diverse characteristics stem from the interplay of charge, spin, lattice, and orbital degrees of freedom at the atomic level. It's no wonder that these materials have garnered attention for their potential to revolutionize everything from computing to energy storage.
◽ Elevating the Paradigm ◽
But we're not just about understanding these properties; we're about mastery. Our research is not confined to merely tuning the inherent traits of these oxides. Instead, we embark on a more audacious journey — controlling these materials at the single atomic layer level. This meticulous level of manipulation allows us to envision and create materials and functionalities that have never before been realized in the world. We are pushing the boundaries of what's possible by synthesizing new forms, conjuring up unprecedented properties, and harnessing their potential in next-generation applications.
