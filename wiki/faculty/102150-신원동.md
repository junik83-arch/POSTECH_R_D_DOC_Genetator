BIRD Lab
(Bio-inspired Robotics and Design Laboratory)
Animals have distinct body structures adapted to their habitats, enabling them to survive and thrive in specific environments. Inspired by this adaptation, the BIRD Lab aims to develop robotic platforms that draw on bio-inspiration to create specialized hardware for different environments.
