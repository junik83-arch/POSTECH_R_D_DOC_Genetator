## 주요성과

(2021) ￭ Development of liquid phase synthesis process of sulfied based solid electrolyte for lithium all-solid-state batteries, Technology Tranfer to company (2021) ￭ Korea’s first development of prismatic high-capacity lithium-ion secondary battery applied with silicon anode material (2011) ￭ Director of Research Center for Lithium Metal Battery Development for Electric Vehicles, Ministry of Science and ICT (2018~2023)
