## 주요성과
- Development of new catalyst materials for fast charging of Li-ion battery, published in Nature. Comm. (2021)
- Development of liquid phase synthesis process of sulfied based solid electrolyte for lithium all-solid-state batteries, Technology Tranfer to company (2021)
- Korea’s first development of prismatic high-capacity lithium-ion secondary battery applied with silicon anode material (2011)
- Director of Research Center for Lithium Metal Battery Development for Electric Vehicles, Ministry of Science and ICT (2018~2023)

Next generation anode materials
The next generation anode material is drawing attention due to the recent increase in demand for lightening secondary batteries and fast charging. The core characteristics of anode material are large capacity, long life, charging and discharging efficiency, and the direction of development of next-generation anode material is attempted to store more lithium ions per unit weight while maintaining the basic anode material structure. Representative attempts are modified graphite, using silicon with high theoretical capacity, or Li metal.
