## 학회발표
아날로그 레이아웃 자동화를 위한 생성형 파운데이션 모델 (제 2회 Korea EDA Workshop); 고속 입출력 회로 테스트 패턴을 위한 병렬화 PRBS 생성기 구현 및 검증 (대한전자공학회 추계학술대회); 3D FeNAND Array Interface Circuits for Compute-in-Memory Applications (22nd International SoC Design Conference-ISOCC); POSTECH's Analog Layout Generation Framework (반도체공학회 하계 학술대회 EDA 전문연구회 워크숍); A Parameterized Cell Design of Resistor Bank (제32회 한국반도체학술대회); Automated Generation of Parallel PRBS Generators in Verilog RTL (제32회 한국반도체학술대회); Layout-to-Generator Conversion Framework with Graphical User Interface for Analog Layout Generators (제32회 한국반도체학술대회); Review: A Speculative Divide-and-Conquer Optimization Method for Large Analog/Mixed-Signal Circuits (20th International SoC Design Conference, ISOCC 2023); A Cost-efficient FPGA-based Embedded System for Biosensor Platform (2022 19th International SoC Design Conference (ISOCC)); A Fast Eye Size Evaluation Method for High Speed Signal (2022 19th International SoC Design Conference (ISOCC)); A Layout Generator of Latch, Flip-Flop, and Shift Register for High-Speed Links (2022 19th International SoC Design Conference (ISOCC)); A 50 Mb/s Full HBC TRX with Adaptive DFE and Variable-Interval 3x Oversampling CDR in 28nm CMOS Technology for A 75 cm Body Channel Moving at 0.75 Cycle/sec (IEEE 48th European Solid-State Circuits Conference (ESSCRIC)); A Study On Reliable High-Speed HBC Enhanced by ECC for Wearable Neural Interfaces (2022 IEEE 4th International Conference on Artificial Intelligence Circuits and Systems (AICAS)); A 20 Gb/s/pin 1.18pJ/b 1149um2 Single-Ended Inverter-based 4-tap Addition-Only Feed-Forward Equalization Transmitter with Improved Robustness to Coefficient Errors in 28nm CMOS (IEEE International Solid-State Circuits Conference (ISSCC)); A 20-Gb/s/pin 0.0024-mm2 Single-Ended DECS TRX with CDR-less Self-Slicing/Auto-Deserialization to Improve Tolerance on Duty Cycle Error and RX Supply Noise for DCC/CDR-less Short-Reach Memory Interfaces (IEEE International Solid-State Circuits Conference (ISSCC))
