1. Course description:
This course was formerly known as Probability Modeling & Analysis (IMEN366). This course covers basic modeling tools to handle stochastic systems such as Markov Chains, Poisson processes, and Continuous-Time Markov Chains. The elementary queueing theory is also covered with a group project.
