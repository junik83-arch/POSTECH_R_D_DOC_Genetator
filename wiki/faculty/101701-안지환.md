## 연구키워드
Energy conversion and storage, Interface engineering, Thin film energy device, Atomic layer process

## 주요성과
￭ Development of ultra-thin flexible capacitor with low temperature plasma atomic layer processes and nanopatterning (record performance for flexible capacitor) ￭ Development of atomic layer deposited anode for methane-fueled SOFC with ultra-low PGM loading ￭ Development of catalytic electrodes with high activity and stability by using atomic layer deposition ￭ Development of thin film sandwich electrolyte for low-temperature SOFC with high ionic conductivity and mechanical stability ￭ Development of MEMS-based low temperature SOFC with record-high power density (1.3 W/cm2 @ 450C)
