Introduction
The CTRL(Control Theory
,
Robotics, and Learning) is a research group of the Department of Electrical Engineering at Pohang University of Science and Technology (POSTECH), Pohang, Republic of Korea. The focus of the laboratory is to develop mathematical control theories and their applications to practical control systems such as robotics, aerospace vehicles, cyber-physical systems, and so on.
