Energy-Efficient Analog IC Design
Topic 1: Energy-Efficient Analog Integrated Circuit (IC) Design
represents the art of achieving high performance with the lowest possible power budget. Analog and mixed-signal circuits form the backbone of sensor interfaces, communication links, and power regulation blocks, making energy efficiency a decisive factor for modern portable and embedded systems. By leveraging advanced circuit techniques—such as optimized biasing, leakage suppression, and subthreshold operation—designers can dramatically extend battery lifetime while minimizing thermal constraints. This paradigm is especially vital for next-generation applications, including IoT nodes, biomedical implants, and self-powered energy-harvesting platforms, where ultra-low-power operation defines system success.
