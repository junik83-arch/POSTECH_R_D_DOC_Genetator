## 연구관심분야
- Understanding the interactions between adult stem cells and their niches
- In vivo lineage tracing for exploring the development and regeneration
- Researches on hair follicle stem cells and melanocyte stem cells
- Generation of skin organoid system

Our lab focuses on understanding the biology of stem cells in the skin, particularly in the hair follicle and epidermis, and their interaction with the stem cell niche. We aim to uncover the mechanisms that regulate cell identity, tissue regeneration, aging, and organ development, using both in vivo models and organoid systems.

Lab Philosophy
"We prioritize questions over techniques."
"We value results that challenge our own models."
"We believe small perturbations can reveal governing principles of complex biological systems."
Research Overview
Classic regenerative biology focuses on stem cell behavior in isolation.
We ask instead:
When a subset of cells changes state, how does that perturb local niches, remodel tissue networks, and amplify regenerative responses?
Our recent work demonstrates that partial reprogramming can propagate widespread repair characteristics across cell types and environments.
Featured Publications
Nature Communications (2026)
We show that mosaic partial cellular reprogramming not only alters targeted epithelial cells but also remodels neighboring microenvironments and immune networks, leading to enhanced wound healing and tissue regeneration.

포스텍 생명과학과·융합대학원 최세규 교수, 최세규 교수님 연구실의 곽민준·조예민 통합과정생, 생명과학과 김종경 교수, 통합과정 최은준 씨 연구팀이 기초과학연구원, 가톨릭대, 미국 워싱턴대와 함께 피부 세포의 '부분적 리프로그래밍'이 주변 세포와 조직 환경을 바꾸어 상처 회복을 촉진하는 원리를 규명했다.
상처 회복이 더딘 당뇨 환자나 고령자를 위한 치료는 물론 항노화 기술과 만성 상처 치료용 의약품 및 기능성 소재 개발에도 중요한 기반이 될 것으로 기대된다.
이 연구는 '네이처 커뮤니케이션즈'에 게재됐다.
