## 연구키워드
Silicon nanoscale and neuromorphic devices, Theoretical modeling and numerical simulation, Renewable energy devices, IoT sensors

## 주요성과
￭ Novel Refreshless capless DRAM(SBDRAM) based on Schottky barrier and Hetero junction ￭ Silicon thermoelectric devices using top-down CMOS process (ZT of 0.345 and 0.308, respectively, at 400K, 2022 world best record) ￭ Highly sensetive hydrogen fluoride gas sensor using 10nm silicon nanosheet FET ￭ Novel Si-based resistive switching devices for synaptic and neuronal functionality ￭ Hourglass-shaped silicon nanowire photodiodes for Lidar, CCTV, image sensor

## 국가전략기술
