## 학회발표
Tri-modal photoacoustic, ultrasound and fluorescence imaging using a transparent ultrasound transducer array (Photons Plus Ultrasound: Imaging and Sensing 2026); An ultraviolet-transparent ultrasound transducer for label-free high-resolution photoacoustic histological imaging (2025 IEEE International Ultrasonics Symposium, IUS 2025); High-performance photoacoustic/ultrasound endoscopy through transparent ultrasound transducer in-vivo preclinical study of live pigs (SPIE PHOTONICS WEST); An ultrasensitive and broadband transparent ultrasound transducer for ultrasound and photoacoustic tomography (Optical Tomography and Spectroscopy); Photoacoustic Imaging of Perfusion Kinematics Using Transparent Ultrasound Transducer and 1064 nm laser (Optical Tomography and Spectroscopy, OT and S 2024 - Part of Optica Biophotonics Congress: Biomedical Optics); A highly sensitive and wideband transparent ultrasound transducer designed for advanced in vivo ultrasound and photoacoustic imaging (Photons Plus Ultrasound: Imaging and Sensing 2024); High-resolution label-free ultraviolet photoacoustic microscopy via an ultraviolet-transparent ultrasound transducer (Photons Plus Ultrasound: Imaging and Sensing 2024); Photoacoustic-only preamplification in simultaneous photoacoustic and ultrasound imaging using switchable preamplifier (Photonics west 2023); Integrated wearable and mobile ultrasound and photoplethysmography device via transparent ultrasound transducer (Photonics west 2023); High-resolution photoacoustic and ultrasound endoscope based on the transparent ultrasound transducer (Photonics west 2023); Tapered Fiber-based Shear Force Scanning Photoacoustic Microscopy (Photons Plus Ultrasound: Imaging and Sensing 2023); High-resolution, high-speed photoacoustic microscopy and photoplethysmography for clinical applications in human fingers (Conference on Photons Plus Ultrasound - Imaging and Sensing); Seamlessly integrated multi-modal imaging system through transparent ultrasound transducer in vivo (Photons Plus Ultrasound: Imaging and Sensing 2021); Continuous blood flow monitoring using patch-type array transducer and portable ultrasound imaging system (Medical Imaging 2021: Ultrasonic Imaging and Tomography); Frequency-dependent calcium dynamics in ultrasound mechanotransduction (SPIE Medical Imaging Conference - Ultrasonic Imaging and Tomography)
