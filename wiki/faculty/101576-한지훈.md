Notice
조회수 2273
포항공과대학교 화학공학과 공정시스템공학 연구실 (PSE) 에서 박사후 연구원을 초빙하고자 하오니 관심 있는 분들의 많은 지원 바랍니다.
연구실 지원방법
1. E-Mail 제출 (jhhan@postech.ac.kr)
2. 제출서류
가. 이력서 (자유양식, 어학성적, 연구업적 및 논문 목록 포함 (해당자 한함)) 1부
나. 성적 증명서, 최종학위 증명서 및 경력증명서 (해당자 한함) 각 1부
3. 자유롭게 컨택 해주세요.
자격 조건
다음 분야 전공자 우대: 화학공학, 에너지공학, 환경공학, 기계공학, 산업공학, 컴퓨터공학 관련 분야
대우 조건
단년/다년 계약가능, 경력에 따라 협의 후 결정

Research Topic 1: Sustainable Biorefinery with Process Design and Systems-level Analysis
Research Goal (연구 목표): 미활용 바이오매스 유래 전 공정 모사 및 모니터링 플랫폼 기술 개발
Our group's research goal is to create a systematic method for sustainable energy process systems design by incorporating various theories and techniques of process systems engineering area and developing a sustainable (economic, low environmental impact, low waste, efficient operation, correct raw material) process flowsheet that can convert the raw materials to the desired products. Its applications that we have been focusing on are synthesis of biomass-to-fuels/Chemicals and CO2&H2-to-fuels/chemicals.
Research Contents & Representative Research Achievements  (연구 내용 및 대표 연구 성과)
2nd (cellulosic) and 3rd (algal) generation biomass resources to produce biofuels and bioproducts
Simulation models to design biorefineries producing high-value building blocks for sustainability
Analysis methods to identify critical factors on economic and environmental aspects in the production process

Research Topic 2 : Waste Materials Recycling with Process Design
Research Goal (연구 목표): 폐 자원으로부터 고부가가치 물질을 회수하기 위한 공정 모사 및 경제 환경적 실현 가능성 분석 플랫폼 개발
Our research group's goal is to develop a systematic approach to designing sustainable energy process systems by integrating various process systems engineering theories and techniques. To achieve this, we aim to develop an environmentally friendly process flow for recovering valuable materials from waste resources. Our primary focus is on the application of recovering chemicals or rare metals from waste plastics, semiconductor wastewater, and Li-ion battery..
