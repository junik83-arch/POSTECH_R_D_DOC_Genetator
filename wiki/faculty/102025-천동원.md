## 주요성과
￭ Discover novel synthetic paradigm of super-hydrogen rich metal-hydrides ￭ Metal-hydrides synthesis in a hydrogen rich conditions using radiation chemistry ￭ Revealing the thermodynamics and kinetics of metal-hydrides with hydrogen absorption/desorption ￭ In-situ characterization of nanomaterials in a liquid, gaseous and high temperature environments
