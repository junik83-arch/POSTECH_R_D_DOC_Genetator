## 연구키워드
Hydrogen Eenergy Materials, Solid-state Hydrogen Storage Systems, Metal-hydrides, Radiation Chemistry, In-situ Characterization

## 주요성과
- Discover novel synthetic paradigm of super-hydrogen rich metal-hydrides
- Metal-hydrides synthesis in a hydrogen rich conditions using radiation chemistry
- Revealing the thermodynamics and kinetics of metal-hydrides with hydrogen absorption/desorption
- In-situ characterization of nanomaterials in a liquid, gaseous and high temperature environments

About
Laboratory for Hydrogen & Energy Materials
Hydrogen & Energy Materials group aims to pioneer new synthetic pathways for metal-hydrides and advanced materials
for next-generation energy systems to achieve carbon zero emissions. By understanding and controlling reactions
between metals and hydrogen (light elements), primarily through in-situ characterization techniques, my research focuses on
1) The synthesis of novel hydrogen energy materials, 2) The discovery of metastable metal-hydrides
3) Hydrogen embrittlement, and 4) Real-time analysis of nanoscale materials.
수소&에너지 소재 연구실은 다양한 실시간 분석기술 (In-situ Characterization)을 활용하여
금속과 수소 (경량 원소) 와의 반응을
이해하고 제어하는 것을 기반으로,
1) 수소 저장을 포함한 수소에너지 신소재 개발, 2)혁신적인 금속 수소화물 (Metal-hydride) 신소재 개발,
3) 금속의 수소 취성 (Hydrogen Embrittlement) 4) 나노스케일 소재 실시간 분석 연구를 수행합니다.
