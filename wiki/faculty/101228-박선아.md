> 박선아 교수는 현재 KAIST 화학과 부교수로 재직 중이며, 2020년부터 2026년까지 POSTECH 화학과에서 조교수 및 부교수를 역임했습니다. 박선아 교수의 연구는 분자, 클러스터, 나노결정이 조립될 때 나타나는 집합적 특성을 연구하는 데 중점을 둡니다. 연구실에서는 재료의 산화환원 활성, 전자/이온 전도성, 자기적 특성을 근본적으로 연구하고, 이를 에너지 저장 및 전기화학 촉매와 같은 응용 분야에 활용합니다. 박선아 교수는 JACS의 편집 자문 위원회와 Inorganic Chemistry Frontiers의 초기 경력 자문 위원회에 참여하고 있습니다.

## 연구관심분야
- Synthesis and characterization of electron- or ion-conductive MOFs
- Energy storage materials
- Electrocatalysts
- Development of solid-state materials based on molecular cluster

## 연구키워드
Inorganic chemistry, Inorganic materials chemistry, Metal-Organic Frameworks, Conductive Materials

## 주요성과
- Synthesis of highly oriented 2D electrically conductive MOF thin films via single-step all-vapor-phase chemical deposition
- Development of solution-processable conductive MOF with acid-dependent chemical and electrical properties
- Studies on the relationship between pore environment and electrochemical performance in conductive MOFs for energy storage applications
- Studies on structure–activity relationships via precise tuning of electrocatalytic active sites in MOFs
- Tuning photophysical properties of gold nanoclusters via lattice symmetry engineering in cluster-based frameworks

RESEARCH
Research in the Park Group is focused on studying the collective properties of molecules, clusters, and nanocrystals when they are assembled.
We are interested in designing materials by predicting their target properties, developing general synthetic principles that allow fine control over assembly, and fundamentally studying the redox activity, electron/ion conductivity, or magnetic properties of the materials synthesized.
Furthermore, we will introduce these new materials into applications such as energy storage and electrochemical catalysts.
