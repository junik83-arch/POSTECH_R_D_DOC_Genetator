Welcome Message
Hello, we are Energy Systems Lab!
In the Laboratory of Smart Energy Systems and Control, we study the integration and control of renewable energy sources such as wind and solar power. Our research focuses on distributed generators, smart buildings, and sustainable energy systems. By combining power system dynamics with data-driven approaches, we aim to contribute to the development of reliable, efficient, and eco-friendly energy infrastructures that support global carbon neutrality.
스마트 에너지 시스템 및 제어 연구실은 풍력 및 태양광과 같은 신재생 에너지의 전력계통 연계 및 제어를 중심으로 연구하고 있습니다. 분산형 발전, 스마트 빌딩, 지속가능한 에너지 시스템을 주요 연구 분야로 하며, 전력 시스템의 물리 기반 모델과 데이터 기반 접근을 결합하여 안정적이고 친환경적인 에너지 인프라 구축에 기여하고자 합니다.

Biography
Youngjin Kim (powersys@)
is currently an associate professor at Pohang University of Science and Technology in the Department of Electrical Engineering. His research fields of interest include distributed generators, smart buildings, and renewable energy resources.
He was a postdoctoral researcher at the Energy Systems Division at Argonne National Laboratory (ANL), where he worked on the projects of self-healing microgrids with Dr. Jianhui Wang from 2015 to 2016. Prior to coming to ANL, he received his Ph.D. degree in Electrical Engineering from Massachusetts Institute of Technology(MIT), where he worked with Professor James L. Kirtley and Professor Leslie K.
