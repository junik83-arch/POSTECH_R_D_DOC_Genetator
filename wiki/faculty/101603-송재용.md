Welcome to our research group, HINT Lab.
HINT Lab at POSTECH starts to pursue the development of functional materials and innovative processes for heterogeneous integration of various semiconductor devices. Our group is focusing on the research of nanotechnology and electrochemistry, for the sake of the applications to advanced packaging of semiconductor devices
and advanced passive/active cooling devices
. Your challenges to our unsolved problems are welcome and your dreams to the challenges will come true with our group discussion and support.

Research
Our group is focusing on the advanced materials and innovative processes for heterogeneous integration (HI) technology that has attracted intensive attention in semiconductor industry.
We are developing
HI tech  through multi-disciplinary researches, in cooperation with other domest
ic and international
rese
arch
groups. HINT Lab is also conducting
new
real-time & in-situ measurement & analysis and industrial applications to electronic packaging, including thermoelectric energy conversion, rechargeable battery, and
e-
sensors, based on nanotechnology and electrochemistry.
Research projects in progress
Electrochemical synthesis of single crystal Cu for 20 nm-scaling interconnect of semiconductor device (NRF-2023.03~2028.02, 700M KRW )
Development of thermal modeling and heat-spreading architecture for heterogeneous integrating package of AI chiplets (IITP-2023.04~2027.12, 9,500M KRW)
Routing design guided development of 3D scaling BEOL interconnect technology (KEIT-2023.04~2027.12, 3,000M KRW)
Electronic transport in ultra-scaling interconnect materials of semiconductor device (Industry-2023.03~2027.02, 180M KRW)
Development of Cu electroplating and thermal/mechanical evaluation technologies for RDL organic substrate (NRF-2023. 06~2027.12, 400M KRW)
Development of equipment, electrolytes, and processes for the simultaneous electroplating of multiscale pattern in advanced semiconductor packages (KEIT-2025.04 ~ 2028.12, 800M KRW)
