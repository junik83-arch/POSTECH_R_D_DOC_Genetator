> 황인석 교수는 포항공과대학교 컴퓨터공학과 소속의 부교수이며, 인간 중심 지능형 시스템 연구실(Human-centered Intelligent Systems Lab)을 이끌고 있습니다. 그의 연구는 인간-컴퓨터 상호작용, 모바일 컴퓨팅, 응용 머신러닝의 교차점에서 AI 기반의 인간 중심 시스템을 탐구하며, 건강 및 웰빙, 가족 및 육아, 노화, 소외 계층 지원 등 다양한 분야에 적용됩니다. 황 교수는 BrainLink 2026에 참여하고 IEEE PerCom 2027의 기술 프로그램 위원회 위원으로 활동하는 등 활발한 대외 활동을 하고 있습니다. 또한, 한국운동영양학회 춘계 학술대회에서 기조연설을 할 예정이며, ACM UIST 2026의 기술 프로그램 위원회 위원으로도 활동하고 있습니다. 그는 2021년부터 컴퓨터 네트워크, 모바일 및 유비쿼터스 컴퓨팅, 지능형 모바일 시스템 등의 과목을 가르치고 있습니다.

Mission
Our group explores novel
AI-infused human-centered systems
that foster computing-rich human life, at the intersection of
human-computer interaction
,
mobile computing
, and
applied machine learning
.
In this light, o
ur research mission is to create novel human-centered intelligent systems and applications to advance real-life human abilities and experiences
, fueled by highly autonomous, cognitive, and ubiquitous agents, yet whose existence mostly stays unobtrusive. Our research is an interdisciplinary ensemble of computing systems, artificial intelligence, connected things, and real-life human factors, e.g., physical, emotional, cognitive & social.
From applications & human-factor perspectives, we disrupt our real-life problems by building and deploying first-of-a-kind AI-powered computing applications in various domains of (but not limited to) health & wellbeing, family & childcare, aging, and supporting underrepresented social groups.
From systems perspective, we think-ahead and design next-generation mobile/edge software platforms to accommodate highly usable machine intelligence and autonomously serve newly emerging real life-driven computing workloads.

Human-AI Interaction & Symbiosis: Hyper-

ersonalization, Actionability, and Priming
AI is rapidly becoming a commodity in every area of computing, and computing is already a commodity of everyday human life. Thus, we think ahead about the next logical step -- AI being a commodity of everyday human life. We explore various embodiments in which
AI boosts everyday human-life agenda
-- family, community, health, social, etc.
Boost for what? The answers are open-ended, but our on-going efforts include
hyper-personalization
to close person-to-person & person-to-population gaps, pursuing
actionability
beyond awareness, and
priming
users to foster better paths without compromising their agency.
One step further, we also investigate the other way --
unique human factors boost everyday AI
. Putting both together, we envision
Human-AI Symbiosis
