## 연구키워드
Chemical biology, Drug discovery, DNA-encoded chemical library, Targeted protein degradation

## 주요성과
￭ Development of macrocyclic peptides/peptidomimetics as therapeutic candidates ￭ Development of DNA-encoded chemical library technologies for the rapid discovery of drug candidates ￭ Development of novel targeted protein degradation technologies based on new E3 ligases
