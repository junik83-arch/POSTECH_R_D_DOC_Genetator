> 이민식 교수는 POSTECH BOIC 4002에 위치한 Tumor Microenvironment and Metabolism Lab(TME Lab)을 이끌고 있습니다. 이 연구실은 암 세포의 변화된 대사 행동을 이해하고, 암 성장 및 생존에 필수적인 대사 기전을 활용하여 암 치료에 기여하는 연구를 수행합니다. 특히 암 국소 환경(TME)의 대사적 특성을 최신 기술과 Multi-Omics를 통해 분석하고, 유전자 조작 동물 모델 및 암 환자 생 검체를 이용하여 핵심 대사 기전을 in vivo에서 확인하며 치료 타깃으로서의 가능성을 평가합니다. 이민식 교수는 2013년부터 PNAS, Nature Communications, Cell Death Differ. 등 저명한 학술지에 다수의 논문을 게재했습니다.

## 연구관심분야
- Deciphering metabolic composition in tumor microenvironment
- Cancer metabolism and behavior
- Metabolic competition/cooperation between cancer cells and other cell types
- Systemic metabolism and cancer behavior

Tumor Microenvironment and Metabolism
Our lab conducts research that are contributing to the treatment of cancer by understanding the altered metabolic behaviors of cancer cells and identifying vital metabolic mechanisms for cancer growth and survival. The understanding of tumor metabolism has gained significant attention and has become the hallmark of cancer in recent decades. However, previous metabolic studies focused on cancer cells have had limitations due to the insufficient consideration of the tumor’s growing environment.
1. Our research team aims to analyze and elucidate the metabolic characteristics of the tumor microenvironment (TME) using cutting-edge technologies and ideas.
2. We observe how various types of cancer adapt to their unique metabolic environments through multi-omics approaches.
3. We further investigate key metabolic mechanisms required for tumor growth and survival in the context of in vivo using state-of-the-art genetically engineered mouse and surgical mouse models, and fresh tissue samples from cancer patients.
Our research encompasses the development of therapies, starting from fundamental research to assess whether these key metabolic mechanisms can be utilized as treatment targets.
